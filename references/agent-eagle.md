[`config/agents.json`](../config/agents.json) at `agents.eagle`.

and actionable Findings. Guard against preference findings, criticism for its

Query `node scripts/zipzap.mjs catalog --kind agents --id eagle` for design or
audit; routine composition loads only its capsule.
